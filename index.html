Health Data Classification — NHANES

XGBoost · LightGBM · CatBoost · Optuna · SHAP

A binary classification pipeline on NHANES health survey data, built during the Summer Analytics 2026 Hackathon (IITG Consulting & Analytics Club). Class imbalance handled with SMOTE, tuning with Optuna, three boosters combined in a soft-voting ensemble, and SHAP used to explain what the model actually keys on. Now being extended into a comparative research paper on explainable ML.

Voice Intent Classifier

Whisper · BERT · PyTorch

Speech in, intent out. Whisper transcribes the audio, a fine-tuned BERT classifier maps the transcript onto intent labels — the front half of any voice-driven assistant.

CNN Image Classifier

PyTorch · CIFAR-10 · Flask

A convolutional network trained on CIFAR-10 on a Colab T4 GPU, then wrapped in a Flask interface so predictions run in a browser rather than a notebook.
